# 09 · Competitor Forensics — Direct Teardown

v1 competitor table was descriptive. This is forensic: what they actually

## A. The AP shrimp majors

### A1. Avanti Feeds / Avanti Frozen Foods

- **Scale (FY24):** Feeds revenue ~₹4,500 cr; Frozen Foods ~₹2,300 cr;
  combined ~₹6,800 cr. Net profit margins ~5-8% (2019 peak ~13%, has
  compressed).
- **Structural strength:** India's only vertically integrated feed +
  shrimp exporter. Feed farmer contracts lock supply + pricing.
- **Structural weakness:**
  - **Single-species (vannamei).** When USA tariff + Ecuador price war
    hit in 2022-25, Avanti's margins compressed from 13% to 5-7%.
  - USA revenue concentration: 45-55% of frozen food exports go to USA.
  - Feed business is EBITDA-positive but capital-intensive; farmer
    advance book is ~₹400+ cr outstanding.
- **2024-25 moves:** exploring Middle East + Japan; trying to raise value-
  added mix; looking at Ecuador outbound sourcing.
- **What we learn:**
  - Scale is not a moat when the core species commoditizes.
  - Geographic diversification is defensive, not offensive.
  - Value-added takes 18-24 months to fill a new line.
- **What we avoid:** single-species single-geography trap. Pillar B + C
  was designed precisely to avoid this.

### A2. Apex Frozen Foods

- **Scale (FY24):** ~₹1,800-2,000 cr revenue (note: v1 had 2,840 which
  includes group; operating company lower). Listed on NSE/BSE.
- **Strength:** India's highest value-added shrimp share (~55% of
  revenue). Raw-material sourcing via both own farms + contract farmers.
- **Weakness:**
  - Debt-heavy balance sheet (D/E ~1.8). Debt service constrains capex +
    new market expansion.
  - Narrow buyer base: Costco, Walmart, Sysco via brokers. Loss of any
    one = material revenue hit.
  - Stock has underperformed peers 2022-25 on margin compression.
- **2024-25 moves:** expanding breaded + cooked shrimp capacity; entering
  Tesco + Aldi via BRC-qualified SKUs.
- **What we learn:**
  - Value-added shift works but requires 5+ years to compound.
  - Debt should not exceed 1.3-1.5× equity for a processor — protects
    against commodity swings.
- **What we avoid:** over-leverage. Our D/E at 1.4× is at the upper
  prudent limit.

### A3. Nekkanti Sea Foods

- **Scale (FY24):** ~₹1,500-1,800 cr revenue; private. Hyderabad HQ.
- **Strength:** Branded line ("Lighthouse") has minor US retail presence;
  HACCP + BAP certified; long-standing Costco relationship.
- **Weakness:**
  - USA concentration (estimated 50%+ of revenue).
  - Limited product diversification (primarily shrimp).
  - Farmer advance structure similar to Avanti but smaller scale.
- **2024-25 moves:** branched into black tiger shrimp from AP + Odisha
  wild catch for Japan.
- **What we learn:** branded line attempt is right but slow + expensive.
- **What we avoid:** over-betting on single retail relationship.

### A4. Devi Sea Foods

- **Scale (FY24):** ~₹1,400-1,600 cr. Private. Vijayawada (AP) HQ.
- **Strength:** Geographic diversification (plants in AP + Tamil Nadu +
  Odisha); reputation for compliance + stability.
- **Weakness:** Lower brand profile; not in US retail directly (via
  importers only).
- **What we learn:** compliance reputation takes 15+ years; it's a
  durable moat once built.

### A5. Sandhya Aqua

- **Scale (FY24):** ~₹450 cr.
- **Strength:** value-added shrimp + domestic retail (Metro/Reliance
  stores).
- **Weakness:** sub-scale; domestic market pricing pressure.

## B. Maharashtra incumbents

### B1. IFB Agro Industries (Seafood Division)

- Parent IFB Agro is listed (NSE/BSE); Seafood division generates ~₹700
  cr.
- **Strength:** modern Vasai plant; UK + EU buyer access; part of IFB
  group's institutional reputation.
- **Weakness:**
  - Sub-scale vs AP majors (on commodity vannamei).
  - High corporate overhead (listed co. structure).
  - Growth has stalled 2019-24 at ₹650-720 cr range.
- **What we learn:** modernity alone isn't enough; need a differentiated
  thesis.
- **What we do differently:** focus on high-margin live + MSC product
  rather than competing commodity scale.

### B2. Coastal Corporation (Mumbai)

- Private; ~₹600 cr revenue; aging plant (1990s commissioned).
- **Strength:** decade+ buyer relationships, especially Japan (squid +
  cuttlefish) and GCC.
- **Weakness:**
  - Aged assets — cold chain + IQF behind modern spec.
  - Technology + labour productivity below modern plants.
  - Margin pressure 2022-25.
- **What we avoid:** buying aged assets via acquisition. Greenfield is
  cleaner.

### B3. Gadre Marine Exports

- Listed (SME); ~₹550 cr; Ratnagiri HQ.
- **Strength:** India's only surimi + analogue-crab maker; global niche
  position.
- **Weakness:**
  - Surimi is capex-intensive + commodity priced.
  - Limited diversification.
- **What we learn:**
  - A niche can be a real moat — Gadre has survived 40 years on one.
  - But a niche that commoditizes (surimi) caps margin upside.
- **What we do differently:** our niches (live + MSC) are more
  differentiated and less commoditizable.

### B4. Magnum Seafoods (Navi Mumbai)

- Mid-scale private; ~₹280 cr.
- **Strength:** flexible contracts + JNPT proximity.
- **Weakness:** no value-added line; commodity positioning.

### B5. Devi Fisheries (Ratnagiri)

- Small family aggregator + exporter.
- Not a strategic competitor; more of a supplier-relationship risk (they
  compete with us for Mirkarwada supply).

## D. Lakshadweep tuna-specific competitors

### D1. Accelerated Freeze Drying Ltd (AFD) — Cochin

- Long-standing Lakshadweep tuna buyer. Freeze-dried + frozen products.
- **Strength:** 25+ years Lakshadweep relationship.
- **Weakness:**
  - Cooch ventures into MSC space are limited.
  - Focus on freeze-dried is niche.
- **What we do differently:** MSC-certified fresh saku/loin is a new
  category for Lakshadweep supply. We don't compete with AFD on freeze-
  dried.

### D2. Amalgam Foods Ltd (Cochin)

- Multi-species seafood exporter; Lakshadweep tuna is one of many
  businesses.
- Not specifically MSC-focused.

### D3. Arya Marine + Mangalore exporters

- Mid-size; commodity tuna focus.

## F. Global benchmark companies (learn-not-beat)

- **Thai Union** ($5 bn, Thailand) — global brand acquisitions (John West,
  Chicken of the Sea); learn M&A + brand building.
- **Mowi** ($6 bn, Norway) — vertical integration salmon; learn Y10+
  playbook.
- **Minh Phu** ($850 mn, Vietnam) — vannamei global scale; our commodity
  filler must be efficient enough not to lose to them.
- **Nueva Pescanova** (Spain) — octopus + squid + farming integration.
  Potential buyer or partner for us long-term.
- **Clearwater Seafoods** (Canada) — premium shellfish; learn live-cargo
  logistics.
- **Sanford Ltd** (NZ) — MSC-certified multi-species; direct model
  parallel.
- **Maruha Nichiro** (Japan) — integrated sogo shosha; our target buyer.
