# 07 · Government Schemes — Capture Plan & Application Mechanics

v1 listed schemes. This doc actually explains how to get the money. Every

## A. Central (Government of India)

### A1. Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) — the flagship

**Administered by:** Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal
Husbandry & Dairying.
**Mode:** 60:40 CS:State (General category states) or 90:10 (NE / Himalayan
/ UTs) for "Central Sector" components; 50:50 CSS for others.

**Activities relevant to us:**

| Component | Subsidy % | Cap | Our relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Modern fish processing unit | 40% (Gen) / 60% (SC/ST/Women) | ₹ based on DPR | **Primary capture**: plant capex ₹15-18 cr × 40% = **₹6-7 cr** |
| Cold chain & storage | 40% / 60% | By component | Secondary — our in-house cold store + reefer |
| Integrated cold chain (multi-product, ≥10 TPD) | Up to 50% | ₹10 cr | Our plant fits this |
| Modernisation of fishing harbours | — | Not ours | (for LCMF partners) |
| Seaweed cultivation | 60% | — | Not Y1-3 |

**What they actually pay for:**
- Civil construction (HACCP-grade)
- IQF + blast freezing equipment
- Cold storage
- Reefer trucks (limited)
- Laboratory
- Packaging line

**What they do NOT pay for:**
- Land
- Working capital
- Pre-operative expenses
- Opex or marketing
- Most IT / software / ERP

**Application mechanics:**
1. DPR preparation by empanelled consultant (cost ₹1.5-2.5 L).
2. Submission to **State Department of Fisheries** (Maharashtra — Office of
   Commissioner of Fisheries, Taraporevala Aquarium, Mumbai).
3. State DoF forwards to PMMSY Central with recommendation.
4. Central sanction → state releases as project progresses.
5. **Disbursal in tranches** (3 usually): foundation, commissioning, first
   commercial operation.
6. Each tranche requires utilisation certificate + site visit.

**Realistic timing:**
- DPR submission to sanction: **6-10 months.**
- First tranche: 3-4 months post-sanction.
- Second tranche: 6-8 months after first.
- Third tranche: 10-14 months after second.
- **Full ₹6-7 cr received by M22-M28 from commissioning, not M12.**

**Traps:**
- Project must be in "Approved Project List" — ensure your DPR hits the
  right component classifications.
- State must have unspent CS allocation; in recent years Maharashtra has
  been a medium-performer on PMMSY utilisation.
- SC/ST/Women-owned component (60% subsidy) requires the principal
  promoter to hold ≥51% and have relevant caste/gender certification. For
  our structure, this is a potential lever if we reorganize promoter
  shareholding.

### A2. Pradhan Mantri Kisan Sampada Yojana (PMKSY) — MoFPI

**Administered by:** Ministry of Food Processing Industries.
**Component relevant:** Integrated Cold Chain & Value Addition Infrastructure.

- Subsidy: 35% for general, 50% for NE + hilly + difficult areas.
- Cap: ₹10 cr.
- Our cold-chain component (reefer fleet + cold storage + pack house) —
  eligible for up to ₹10 cr × 35% = ₹3.5 cr.
- **Overlap check:** Cannot double-dip PMMSY + PMKSY on the same asset
  line. Partition required (PMMSY for processing hall + IQF; PMKSY for
  reefer fleet + pack house + cold stores).

**Application:** MoFPI portal + State Nodal agency (MIDC
representation or MoFPI Area Manager office in Pune).
**Timing:** 8-12 months sanction; 4 tranches over ~18 months.

### A3. MNRE rooftop solar subsidy

- Administered by MNRE via state DISCOM (MSEDCL in our case).
- Subsidy structure updated 2024: **30% capex subsidy for up to 3 kW; 20%
  for next 7 kW; beyond 10 kW no subsidy but net-metering available.**
- For a 1.2 MWp commercial/industrial rooftop, direct subsidy is limited.
  **Realistic MNRE direct subsidy: ₹35-50 L, not ₹1 cr.**
- However: **accelerated depreciation** (80% in Y1, Y2) + **MSEB net-
  metering credit** economically similar to ~15% effective capex rebate.
- Process: empanelled EPC installs, RE Department inspects, subsidy flows
  through DISCOM.
- Timing: 6-12 months post-commissioning.

### A4. NABARD Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)

- Administered by NABARD.
- 3% interest subvention on term loan up to ₹2 cr; subvention paid
  direct to lending bank.
- Credit guarantee up to ₹2 cr via CGTMSE.
- Application via lending bank (SBI, Bank of Baroda, PNB etc.).
- **Effective saving:** on ₹2 cr × 3% × 7 years = ₹42 L interest saving;
  real terms ~₹25-30 L NPV.

### A5. Interest subvention via Kisan Credit Card (KCC-Fishery)

- Only for fisher-folk supplier side, not us directly.
- **Leverage strategy:** we connect our AP pond suppliers to KCC via their
  FPO; they get working-capital finance at 7% vs 11-13% informal credit.
- This gives us indirect supply-lock benefit (our FPO-linked farmers are
  committed to us for a season).

### A6. RoDTEP (Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products)

- DGFT, all exports.
- Rate for frozen seafood: **0.7-1.7%** of FOB value (species-specific,
  revised periodically).
- Credit via electronic duty scrip usable for import duty payment or
  transferable.
- Target: capture 1.5% weighted-average across our product mix.

### A7. Service Export from India Scheme (SEIS)

- Applicable to very specific seafood service exports (not our processing).
- Not relevant in our case.

### A8. Export Promotion Capital Goods (EPCG)

- DGFT; zero-duty import of capital goods against export-earning
  obligation.
- **Value trap:** must fulfil export obligation = 6× duty-saved within 6
  years. Non-compliance = pay duties with interest.
- Strategically useful for our IQF import (Frigoscandia/Carnitech) and
  HPLC-MS/MS. Estimated duty saving: ₹40-60 L; EO achievable.

### A9. Assistance to States for Developing Export Infrastructure (TIES)

- DoC/DGFT; state-level export cluster infrastructure.
- We apply via Maharashtra State Export Promotion Corporation.
- Can fund reefer truck parking bay, common cold-storage extension, etc.

### A10. MPEDA-administered schemes

- **MPEDA Export Infrastructure Assistance**: part-funds pre-shipment
  cold chain, QC equipment; 20-30% subsidy.
- **MPEDA BAP + MSC co-funding**: partial support for certification
  costs for first-time applicants.
- **MPEDA Buyer-Seller Meet** participation funding.
- **Production-linked incentive (PLI)**: MoFPI's PLI for Food
  Processing — we qualify if branded product revenue crosses ₹50 cr
  threshold. Incentive is 4-10% of sales. Relevant Y4+.

### A11. SIDBI / Stand-Up India / MUDRA

- For early-stage funding gap: SIDBI's Sustainable Finance Scheme for
  renewable + processing; Stand-Up India for SC/ST/Women promoters.
- MUDRA up to ₹10 L working-capital; too small for us except for vendor
  development.

## B. Maharashtra State Schemes

### B1. Maharashtra Industrial Policy 2024

- **D-zone classification:** Purandar taluka in Pune district.
- **Benefits:**
  - 7-year SGST refund up to 100% of eligible investment
  - Electricity duty exemption (saves ~₹0.8-1.2/unit)
  - Stamp duty exemption on land/building (up to ₹1 cr value)
  - Power tariff subsidy (₹2/unit for 3 years, capped)
  - Interest subsidy: 5% on term loan for 7 years (cap ₹50 L/year)
- Application via Directorate of Industries or MIDC portal.
- Timing: 3-6 months sanction.

### B2. Maharashtra State Fisheries Policy

- Capex subsidy (top-up to PMMSY): up to ₹40 L per project.
- Cold chain assistance: ₹10-15 L for reefer + ice plant.
- Application via Commissionerate of Fisheries.

### B3. Mahila Udyog Aadhaar / Women Entrepreneur Benefits

- State-level additional subsidies if at least 51% woman-owned.
- Additional 10% capex top-up under certain schemes.

### B4. Pune Zilla Parishad — Agri + Allied

- Local-level schemes for infrastructure development (access roads,
  water pipeline extensions).
