# 02 · Sharpened Thesis — Option B + C, Rebuilt

Cutting the hedging. This is the actual winning business we're building.

## The one-sentence thesis

> **"India's first investor-grade live-seafood and MSC-certified pole-and-
> line tuna specialist, selling to premium Asian HoReCa and Japanese/European
> sashimi buyers, with commodity vannamei processing as a cash-flow filler —
> not a strategy."**

## The two pillars

### Pillar B — Live Seafood to Asia Premium HoReCa

- **Products:** Live spiny lobster (*Panulirus homarus + P. polyphagus* from
  Konkan rocky reefs); live mud crab (*Scylla serrata + S. olivacea*, initially
  aggregated from West Bengal + Konkan creeks, later farmed); secondary
  product: chilled whole pomfret (3-5 day shelf to Singapore + GCC air).
- **Buyers:** 5-star hotel procurement in Hong Kong, Singapore, Tokyo (+
  Macau, KL, Bangkok secondary); specialty wet-market importers in HK's
  Sai Ying Pun fish market; Tsukiji-successor distributors in Toyosu.
- **Why we win:**
  - Geography — PNQ/BOM → DXB → HKG is 16-18 hrs door-to-door, beating
    Sri Lanka (weaker infra) and Myanmar (regulatory unreliable).
  - Konkan rocky reefs have high-quality lobster populations, currently
    aggregated and sold frozen-tail at ~₹1,100-1,300/kg. Live realisation
    is ₹2,400-2,900/kg. We capture that delta.
  - No Indian incumbent does live-export at investor-grade scale from
    Maharashtra. Closest analog: small Chennai/Cochin traders at 5-10 MT/yr
    each; and a handful of aggregators.
- **Unit economics (target):**
  - Lobster live: FOB $28-34/kg · gross margin 28-34% · mortality cost
    budgeted at 10-12% · target tonnage Y3 = 60 MT = ₹16-20 cr revenue
  - Mud crab live: FOB $15-18/kg · gross margin 24-30% · target tonnage
    Y3 = 80 MT = ₹11-14 cr revenue
  - Chilled pomfret air: FOB $12-15/kg · gross margin 18-22% · target
    tonnage Y3 = 140 MT = ₹14-17 cr revenue
- **Defensibility:**
  - Carrier relationships (Emirates SkyCargo live-animal protocol + Qatar
    Cargo) require 12+ months to build, 6 months to re-build
  - Buyer trust on live cargo is stickier than frozen — one bad shipment
    and the relationship dies; conversely, 24 clean months and switching
    cost is massive
  - Konkan boat-owner relationships take 18-24 months to build

### Pillar C — MSC-Certified Pole-and-Line Tuna + Premium Cephalopods

- **Products:**
  - **Yellowfin tuna** saku block, CO-free loin for EU/Japan, frozen whole
    for US poke-bowl market; sourced from **Lakshadweep pole-and-line
    fishery** (already MSC pre-assessment positive).
  - **Octopus** (*Octopus aegina + O. cyanea*) from Konkan + Lakshadweep
    waters, pre-cooked and sliced for Spain/Italy HoReCa.
  - Secondary: skipjack tuna for canning-grade export to Thai canners (cash
    flow filler), cuttlefish for Japan.
- **Buyers:**
  - **Japan:** Mitsubishi Corporation, Maruha Nichiro, Nissui, Kyokuyo — big
    4 sogo shosha with tuna divisions.
  - **EU:** Caladero (Spain), Nueva Pescanova (Spain), Scandic Pelagic
    (octopus). UK/Ireland specialty: M&S Food.
  - **USA:** MSC-premium retailers — Whole Foods Market, Trader Joe's,
    Costco (through broker); and sashimi distributors like True World
    Foods + ABS Seafood.
- **Why we win:**
  - MSC certification premium is real and durable: +25-35% over non-MSC
    for yellowfin tuna; higher for octopus.
  - Lakshadweep pole-and-line fishery is environmentally differentiated
    (no bycatch, no FAD-set PS/LL methods) — genuine sustainability story
    rather than greenwash.
  - India's existing tuna exporters (Accelerated Freeze Drying, Amalgam
    Foods, Arya Marine) are commodity-focused without MSC certification —
    we'd be India's first.
- **Unit economics (target):**
  - Yellowfin saku/loin: FOB $14-22/kg · gross margin 28-35% · Y3 tonnage
    250 MT = ₹30-38 cr
  - Octopus cooked/sliced: FOB $8.5-11.5/kg · gross margin 26-32% · Y3
    tonnage 450 MT = ₹32-42 cr
  - Cuttlefish + skipjack combined: FOB $4.5-7/kg · Y3 250 MT = ₹10-14 cr
- **Defensibility:**
  - MSC chain-of-custody certification: 18-24 months to achieve, ₹35-45 L
    audit + consulting cost, annual re-certification. Real barrier.
  - Lakshadweep supply relationship: LCMF + boat-owner associations need
    18 months to build + ₹80 L boat-upgrade advance to secure allocations.

### The cash-flow filler (not strategy)

- **Vannamei HLSO 31/40 for commodity export** to China + SEA LC-at-sight
  markets. Fills IQF capacity off-peak, turns working capital, keeps power
  factor high on the solar inverter.
- **Target: 700-900 MT/yr at 6-9% gross margin.** We do NOT compete
  with Avanti/Apex on this. We do not push into US retail with it. It is
  literally cash-flow plumbing.

## Three kill-conditions

If any of these is true at Y2, this thesis is dead and we exit cleanly:

1. **MSC pre-assessment fails for Lakshadweep fishery.** Without MSC, Pillar
   C premium collapses and thesis falls back to commodity tuna.
2. **Live-cargo mortality averages >15% for 2 consecutive quarters.**
   Reliability kills the HK relationship permanently.
3. **Farm-gate vannamei crosses ₹520/kg for 3 months (Ecuador price war
   intensifies beyond forecasts).** Cash-flow filler becomes cash-flow
   drain; need to mothball vannamei line.

## What we are NOT doing

- Not chasing US retail in Y1-Y3. Too long a USFDA cycle, too competitive,
  too tariff-exposed.
- Not building a "Konkan brand." 10-year project, save equity for it later.
- Not scaling into 16 species. Three core species plus two optional
  (cephalopods mix + chilled pomfret).
- Not competing with Avanti/Apex/Nekkanti on their home turf of commodity
  US shrimp.
- Not doing cage-culture in Y1-Y2. Too much capex + 18-24 month payback
  delay before the core business is stable.
