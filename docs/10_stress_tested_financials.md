## D. DSCR (debt service coverage) — reality-check

Term loan ₹16 cr @ 9.5%, 10-yr, 24-mo moratorium.
- Annual debt service (post-moratorium): ₹2.6 cr principal + ~₹1.3 cr
  interest Y3 = **₹3.9 cr**.

| Year | EBITDA | Debt service | DSCR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Y1 | (240) | 120 (int only) | N/A — moratorium |
| Y2 | 680 | 150 (int only) | 4.5× (principal starts Y3) |
| Y3 | 2,790 | 390 | 7.2× |
| Y4 | 3,940 | 380 | 10.4× |
| Y5 | 4,895 | 370 | 13.2× |

**Bank DSCR minimum 1.5×** → base case comfortably passes.

Bear-case Y3 DSCR: 1,150 / 390 = 2.95× — still passes.

### But: Y2 cash-flow pinch

- Y2 EBITDA ₹680 L vs Y2 interest ₹150 L = positive.
- Y2 WC absorption: inventory + receivables ramp ~₹8-9 cr vs v1's ₹5 cr.
- **Y2 is the cash-flow bottleneck year.** Needs promoter-equity
  injection (₹1-2 cr) or CC utilisation at peak.

## G. Kill conditions (restated, with numbers)

If any of the following trigger, thesis is materially damaged; exit or
re-plan:

1. **MSC fishery assessment fails for Lakshadweep by M18.** Pillar C
   collapses to commodity tuna. Thesis needs rebuild within 3 months.
2. **Trailing 6-month live-cargo mortality >14%.** Pillar B unit economics
   break. Needs investigation, carrier change, or exit from live.
3. **Y2 EBITDA <₹300 L (vs base ₹680).** Cash-flow stress → additional
   promoter top-up OR mothball value-added line.
4. **Combined-shock Y3 IRR <12%.** Reconsider project — may still exit at
   equity breakeven.
5. **Any RASFF antibiotic positive event in first 18 months.** EU access
   compromised 2-3 years. Rebuild needed.
