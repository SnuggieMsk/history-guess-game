# 01d · Financial Teardown — Where the Numbers Don't Survive Stress

v1 gives a clean-looking 5-year P&L: ₹155 cr revenue, 12.6% EBITDA, 24.2%
project IRR, 4.6-yr payback. An experienced investor picks this apart in 15
minutes. Here's the list.

## 1. The revenue ramp is aggressive.

v1: Y1 ₹42 cr → Y2 ₹76 cr → Y3 ₹98 cr → Y4 ₹127 cr → Y5 ₹155 cr.

Investor question: **"When do you have USFDA establishment approval?"**
Answer in v1: M18. Investor: "The typical USFDA audit + approval cycle for
a new establishment is 12-24 months after first request, not 6 months post-
commissioning. Your realistic first US retail revenue is M24-M30, not Y1."

Effect: Y1 revenue should assume zero US. Realistic Y1 composition:
- GCC ethnic retail via Dubai trader — ₹7-10 cr
- China wholesale LC at sight — ₹6-9 cr
- SEA LC — ₹3-5 cr
- Domestic retail / institutional — ₹2-4 cr

**Realistic Y1: ₹20-28 cr, not ₹42 cr.** Pushed revenue back 6-9 months
shifts break-even year from Y2 to Y3. Cash flow gap is ₹6-8 cr.

## 2. Gross margin on commodity vannamei is optimistic.

v1 blends 11.5% on commodity shrimp + 18-24% on value-added to get 12.6%
Y5 EBITDA. But:

- Ecuador + Vietnam are flooding vannamei market. Actual 2024-2026 Indian
  FOB spot for vannamei HLSO 31/40 is US$5.40-6.80/kg, not $7.0.
- At $5.90 FOB = ₹513/kg, and our cost stack of ₹514/kg, we're **break-even
  to slightly loss-making on commodity vannamei.**
- v1's "value-added mix shift to 30% by Y5" is doable, but **getting there
  requires winning private-label contracts we don't yet have a buyer pipeline
  for.** Every value-added line we add takes 12-18 months to fill capacity.

## 3. Working capital is underestimated.

v1: 12 days inventory + 35 days receivables - 18 days payables = 29-day
cycle.

Reality:
- Receivables on US/EU open account are **45-90 days** (30-60 LC sight is
  rare for new suppliers; they get OA 60-90 for year 1-2).
- Ethnic retail + GCC have 45-day average.
- Inventory often builds 20-30 days during off-season farm cycles.
- Supplier payables: AP farmers want advances or cash-at-purchase; 18-day
  credit is fiction for non-anchor suppliers.

**Realistic WC cycle: 55-70 days, not 29 days.** That's ₹11-15 cr NWC at Y3
revenue, not ₹8 cr. Additional CC limit of ₹6 cr or promoter top-up needed.

## 4. Project IRR of 24% is fragile.

Run three shocks:

| Shock | Effect on IRR |
|-------|---------------|
| USA tariff persists (no revenue recovery post-Y2) | IRR drops to 14-16% |
| Vannamei FOB 10% lower (Ecuador price war continues) | IRR drops to 17-19% |
| INR appreciates to ₹78/USD (from ₹87) | IRR drops to 12-14% |
| All three combined (moderate stress) | IRR drops to 6-9% — **project loses money on NPV** |

v1 shows only single-variable sensitivity. A real investor asks for
combined scenarios.

## 5. Subsidy capture is assumed but not scheduled.

v1 books ₹5.2 cr subsidy. But:
- **PMMSY capex subsidy comes in 3 tranches** (40% on foundation, 30% on
  commissioning, 30% on first commercial operation). Each tranche requires
  state-level DPR sign-off, field audit, and utilization certificate.
  Typical disbursement delay: **8-14 months from eligibility trigger.**
- **PMKSY on cold-chain requires MoFPI approval** via state nodal. Another
  6-12 month cycle.
- **MNRE solar subsidy** is now dispensed via DISCOM rebate (not direct
  transfer), effective only after net-metering is live.

Cash-flow reality: of ₹5.2 cr headline subsidy, we see ~₹1.8 cr in Y1,
~₹2.2 cr in Y2, ~₹1.2 cr in Y3. v1 books all of it in Y1.

## 6. Debt service is tight.

v1: ₹14 cr term loan @ 9.5% over 7 years with 1-yr moratorium.
EMI post-moratorium ~₹2.35 cr/yr principal + ~₹1.25 cr/yr interest.
Total DS ~₹3.6 cr in Y2.

Y2 EBITDA (v1): ₹4.2 cr.
Y2 DSCR: **4.2 / 3.6 = 1.17x.** Banks want 1.5x minimum.

If realistic Y2 revenue is ₹40-50 cr (not ₹76 cr) and margin is 5-7% (not
5.5%), Y2 EBITDA = ₹2-3 cr, DSCR = 0.6-0.8x. **We miss debt service in Y2.**

Mitigation options:
- 2-year moratorium instead of 1
- Promoter top-up to ₹14-16 cr (reduce term loan size)
- Slower capex drawdown to push depreciation + interest burden into Y3+

## 7. Exit / residual value is not addressed.

v1 doesn't answer: "What's the business worth at Y5? Who'd acquire it?
What's the strategic buyer profile?"

For seafood companies, trade-buyer valuations are typically 5-7x EBITDA or
1.2-1.8x revenue. Y5 EBITDA ₹19.5 cr → valuation ₹97-137 cr. Promoter
equity of ₹10 cr becomes ~₹70-90 cr after debt adjustment — **6-8x in 5
years at base case.** Not bad if execution holds, but reliant on all
assumptions going right. More realistic pessimistic case: 2-3x.

## 8. Capex contingency is too thin.

v1 has ₹75 L pre-ops + soft costs, about 3% of gross capex. Industry norm
is 8-12% contingency on first greenfield. Add ₹1.2-1.8 cr.

## What the investor teardown produces

An honest financial view for the same project, under Option B+C thesis, with
realistic inputs:

- **Y5 revenue: ₹110-140 cr** (narrower thesis, higher margin per kg)
- **Y5 EBITDA: 14-17%** (because we're not playing commodity vannamei)
- **Y5 PAT: 7-9%**
- **Project IRR: 16-22%** (not 24%)
- **Equity IRR: 22-28%** (not 31%)
- **Realistic payback: 5-6 years** (not 4.6)
- **Promoter equity requirement: ₹12-14 cr** (not ₹10 cr)
- **Project cost: ₹36-40 cr** (not ₹32 cr)

These are less glamorous numbers but defensible under stress.
