# 06 · Certifications — The Access Gates

Each buyer segment is gated by certifications. This doc maps every cert
we need, timeline, cost, and what it unlocks. Without these, none of the
buyer-directory relationships move past trial.

---

## A. Mandatory from Day 1 (India domestic + minimum export)

### A1. MPEDA registration (RCMC)

- **Marine Products Export Development Authority** registration cum
  membership certificate (RCMC).
- Prerequisite: company incorporation + IEC code + FSSAI + Factory Act
  licence + PCB consent to operate.
- Fee: ₹25,000 + annual ~₹10,000.
- Timeline: 30-45 days post-application once documents complete.
- Unlocks: all seafood exports (without this = no export).

### A2. HACCP certification

- **Hazard Analysis & Critical Control Points** — MPEDA-recognised
  certification.
- Prerequisite: HACCP manual + CCP documentation + GMP + SSOP + trained
  HACCP team (minimum 5 people, 1 internal lead auditor).
- Fee: ₹1-1.5 L initial audit + ₹40-60 k annual surveillance.
- Timeline: 60-90 days (plant must be commissioned, trial runs complete).
- Unlocks: MPEDA export certification per-shipment.

### A3. FSSAI (domestic + food export)

- Central Licence (high-risk food manufacturer).
- Fee: ₹7,500/year.
- Timeline: 30-60 days.
- Unlocks: legal processing + labelling in India.

### A4. Factory Act + Labour

- Under Maharashtra Factories Rules + Shops & Establishments.
- Fee: one-time registration ~₹15,000 + annual fees.
- Timeline: 30-45 days.

### A5. PESO (ammonia refrigerant licence)

- Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation — required because IQF +
  cold store use ammonia (R717).
- Fee: ₹25-50 k + safety audit + trained operator certification.
- Timeline: 90-120 days.

### A6. Pollution Control Board (Maharashtra PCB)

- Consent to Establish + Consent to Operate.
- Critical because we're in a water-stressed district with ZLD
  obligation.
- Fee: proportional to capital (₹50 k-₹2 L).
- Timeline: 90-120 days.

## B. Destination-country regulatory (gating exports)

### B1. USFDA Establishment Registration + Prior Notice

- **Food Facility Registration (FFR)** — all facilities exporting food to
  US.
- Free, online, biennial renewal.
- **Prior Notice** — per-shipment notification to USFDA.
- Free, required per-shipment.
- **Seafood HACCP regulation 21 CFR 123** — compliant plant required.
- Audit: USFDA may conduct unannounced facility audit; typical 1-3 years
  from first approach.
- **Critical step: LAAF (Laboratory Accreditation for Analyses of Foods)**
  for our lab if we want automatic acceptance of our test results.
- **Realistic timeline for "automatic green-lane" status: 24-30 months
  from first export to US.**

### B2. EU Approval Number

- Issued via MPEDA (as India's competent authority); published in EU's
  RASFF list of approved establishments.
- Requires:
  - Plant SSOP compliant with EU Commission Regulation 852/2004 + 853/2004
  - HACCP to EU standards
  - Competent Authority (MPEDA) certificate of site audit
- Timeline: **12-18 months** post-commissioning for a new establishment.
- Fee: MPEDA charges ~₹1 L per audit cycle.

### B3. Japan — Registered Establishment

- **Japan Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW)** registration.
- Easier than EU/US if MPEDA + HACCP in place.
- Timeline: 3-6 months from application.
- Key: pre-notification 2-3 days before each shipment, sample acceptance
  protocol.

### B4. China — GACC Registration

- **General Administration of Customs of China** facility registration.
- Required for all seafood to China since 2021 (Regulation 248).
- Timeline: 6-12 months.
- Critical: China audits have tightened post-COVID; facility-level
  rejection common for documentation lapses.

### B5. GCC countries

- UAE + Saudi + Oman + Qatar all accept MPEDA certification without
  separate facility registration (if HACCP + MPEDA stamp on each
  shipment).
- Some markets require Halal certification for seafood (Gulf Halal
  Centre Dubai, or local equivalent).
- Halal fee: ~₹60-80 k annual + per-shipment declaration.

### B6. Hong Kong + Singapore

- HK: AFCD import permit + FEHD clearance. Straightforward.
- SG: SFA (Singapore Food Agency) permit; mandatory Heat Treatment
  Certificate or cooking declaration for some species.

### B7. Korea

- KFDA (MFDS) registration required since 2018.
- 3-6 month timeline.

## C. Voluntary / premium certifications (unlock margin)

### C1. BAP (Best Aquaculture Practices) — 2-star / 3-star / 4-star

- Issued by Global Seafood Alliance.
- 2-star: farm + processor
- 3-star: farm + processor + hatchery
- 4-star: farm + processor + hatchery + feed mill
- Audit cost: ₹2-4 L per facility per year.
- Timeline: 3-6 months from application.
- Unlocks: Walmart, Sysco, Costco, Whole Foods (some buyers mandate BAP
  minimum).

### C2. ASC (Aquaculture Stewardship Council)

- For farmed shrimp + salmon + tilapia + trout etc.
- Stricter than BAP on environmental impact.
- Audit cost: ₹3-5 L per facility per year.
- Timeline: 12-18 months (first time).
- Unlocks: Whole Foods, Aldi, Tesco premium tiers, German retail
  (Metro, Rewe).

### C3. MSC (Marine Stewardship Council) — wild-caught

- **Critical for Pillar C.**
- Two components:
  - **Fishery certification** — covers the wild fishery itself
    (Lakshadweep pole-and-line tuna). Requires 2-3 year full assessment
    (~₹80 L-₹1.2 cr cost, typically shared by multiple fishery
    participants + MPEDA).
  - **Chain of Custody (CoC) certification** — covers our plant.
    Required to label product as MSC. ₹2-3 L/year.
- **Lakshadweep pole-and-line fishery MSC status:** Currently not MSC-
  certified (as of our latest research). Maldives pole-and-line skipjack
  is MSC-certified since 2012 — strong precedent that Lakshadweep could
  follow.
- **Our role:** we can either (a) initiate/fund the Lakshadweep MSC
  fishery assessment in partnership with LCMF + MPEDA + DoF (₹20-30 L our
  contribution over 2-3 years), or (b) wait for an industry consortium.
  **Recommendation: co-fund + actively participate. This is a defining
  moat for us.**
- Unlocks: Whole Foods, Costco premium, Trader Joe's, Japan MSC-premium
  buyers, EU retail premium tiers. **Commands +25-35% pricing.**

### C4. BRC / IFS / SQF (retailer-driven)

- BRC (British Retail Consortium) — gates UK Tesco/Sainsbury's/M&S.
- IFS (International Featured Standards) — gates German/French retail.
- SQF (Safe Quality Food) — gates some US retail.
- Audit cost: ₹3-5 L each per year (BRC most expensive).
- Timeline: 6-9 months first time.

### C5. Halal + Kosher

- Halal: Gulf markets, UK/US diaspora.
- Kosher: Orthodox/Jewish markets. Specialty (OU certification).

### C6. Organic / Wild-Organic

- For farmed: "Organic" requires USDA/EU-equivalent organic-feed
  validation.
- Very few Indian farms qualify today.
- Premium of 15-25% where buyers care.

## D. Environmental + social

### D1. Sedex / SMETA

- Social accountability audit (labour conditions, factory welfare).
- Often buyer-requested (Tesco, Marks & Spencer).
- Cost: ₹1-2 L; annual renewal.

### D2. FSSC 22000

- Food Safety Management System; ISO 22000 + ISO/TS 22002-1.
- More rigorous than basic HACCP; preferred by large buyers.
- ₹3-5 L/year.

### D3. ISO 14001 (environmental)

- Environmental management; some EU buyers expect.
- ₹1.5-2 L/year.

### D4. SA 8000 (social accountability)

- Rigorous labour standard. A step above Sedex.
- Some high-end EU retail requires.

## E. Chain-of-custody for origin/story claims

- **GI (Geographical Indication)** for specific Konkan products (future
  play). No GI exists today for Konkan seafood; could be established.
- **Fishery Improvement Project (FIP)** participation: halfway house to
  MSC for wild fisheries; cost ₹5-10 L/year; demonstrates sustainability
  trajectory to premium buyers who can't wait for full MSC.

## F. Our certification roadmap (Y1-Y5)

| Period | Certifications to hold |
|---|---|
| Pre-commissioning (M-6 to M12) | IEC, FSSAI, MPEDA-RCMC, Factory Act, PCB, PESO |
| Commissioning (M12-M15) | HACCP, Halal |
| Y1-Y2 (M15-M36) | BRC, BAP 2-star, FSSC 22000, HK/SG/UAE/China GACC |
| Y2-Y3 (M24-M48) | USFDA LAAF, EU Establishment, ASC, Japan MHLW, SA 8000 |
| Y3-Y5 (M36-M60) | **MSC CoC + Lakshadweep MSC fishery co-funding**, ISO 14001, Sedex |

Annual recurring cert cost at Y3 steady state: **₹28-35 L/year**.
One-off lab accreditation + cert cost by Y3: **₹85-120 L**.
Co-funding MSC fishery assessment: **₹20-30 L over Y1-Y3**.

These costs are retained as part of financial model stress testing
(see `10_stress_tested_financials.md`).
