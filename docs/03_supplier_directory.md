## A. Konkan coast (Maharashtra)

### A1. Ratnagiri district

**Mirkarwada Jetty & Fish Landing Centre (Ratnagiri town)**
- Location: Pethkilla, Ratnagiri 415612. Coordinates ~17.001°N, 73.273°E.
- Auction: dawn (4:30 AM), operated by the municipal fisheries committee,
  with a dedicated auctioneer per aisle (historically a lineage trade —
  "flamboyant" auction style noted in trade press).
- Daily arrivals: 20-40 MT peak (Oct-May), 5-12 MT monsoon-lean (Jun-Jul
  closed by trawl ban).
- Principal species landed: silver pomfret, Indian mackerel, ribbonfish,
  squid, lobster (November-February peak), sardines, tuna (longline
  vessels), Bombay duck, crab.
- Boats registered: ~800 (mix of small-mechanised, purse-seine, trawl,
  gillnet).
- Access path for a new entrant:
  1. Register as a posted-buyer with Ratnagiri District Fisheries Office.
  2. Hire a dedicated dock-side buyer (tonnage-basis commission, typically
     2-3%).
  3. Place pre-auction standing orders with 3-4 named boat-owner families
     (see below).
  4. Post a refundable deposit (~₹5-10 L) with the market committee to
     secure auction-priority lane.
- **Known aggregators at Mirkarwada:**
  - **SKR Exports India Pvt Ltd** (owner: Suresh Kumar Khadilkar) — based at
    Shivshakti Ice Factory, Bhagvati Bandar Road, Mirkarwada; active
    exporter/aggregator, uses ice-factory infrastructure for first-cooling.
    **Status for us:** competitor + potential co-buyer relationship.
  - **Universal Trading Company (Ratnagiri)** — pomfret + silver pomfret
    specialist, sources Mirkarwada + Harnai. Mid-size.
  - A cluster of 6-10 family aggregator-traders who take 4-7% margin
    between auction and exporter; we either go around them (direct
    boat-owner contracts) or partner.

**Harne (Harnai) Fish Landing, Dapoli taluka**
- Location: Harnai, Dapoli, Ratnagiri 415714. Coordinates ~17.817°N,
  73.096°E.
- Distinctive: unique bullock-cart-to-auction transport at low tide (tourist
  draw, operational constraint). Auction twice daily (post-haul).
- Daily arrivals: 10-18 MT peak (Oct-May).
- Principal species: small shrimp, pomfret, ribbonfish, crab, **lobster
  (high quality, rocky-reef specimens)**, mackerel.
- Boats: ~400 smaller craft.
- Access: posted-buyer license via Dapoli taluka fisheries office.
  Aggregator landscape less mature than Mirkarwada — easier for a new
  entrant to get direct relationships.
- **Target: lock in 30-40% of lobster landings via boat-owner forward
  contracts** — payable 50% on catch, 50% on delivery; ₹150/kg advance
  to secure Nov-Feb season.

**Jaigad + Dabhol landings (smaller)**
- Ratnagiri district; smaller purpose-built jetties; 3-8 MT/day each.
- Good for supplementary pomfret + squid.

### A2. Sindhudurg district

**Devgad Landing Centre (Devgad taluka)**
- Location: Devgad, Sindhudurg 416613.
- Daily arrivals: 8-15 MT peak.
- Principal species: **lobster (premium rocky-reef specimens), octopus,
  squid, pomfret.** Small boats, gillnet + handline dominant.
- Boats: ~300.
- Access: Sindhudurg District Fishermen's Co-operative Federation; direct
  boat-owner relationships possible due to small community.

**Malvan Landing Centre (Malvan taluka)**
- Location: Malvan, Sindhudurg 416606.
- Specialty: **octopus** (cyanea + aegina from rocky shores); excellent
  squid; some lobster.
- Boats: ~250; artisanal + small-mechanised mix.
- Access: Malvan Fishermen's Co-operative Society. The society is also
  involved in eco-tourism (Tarkarli); there's social goodwill for
  sustainability-focused buyers.

**Vijaydurg + Achra smaller jetties**
- 2-5 MT/day each; crab + small shrimp + mixed wild fish.

### A3. Raigad district

**Karanja Landing (Uran taluka)**
- Location: Karanja, Uran, Raigad 400704. Adjacent to JNPT.
- Daily arrivals: 20-35 MT.
- Principal species: wild shrimp, cuttlefish, squid, pomfret, mackerel.
- Critical: **nearest Maharashtra landing to JNPT container terminal** —
  8 km from port. Direct advantage for our reefer-container export.
- Access: Raigad Fishermen's Co-operative + Karanja Jetty Trust. Mumbai
  aggregators dominate; new entrant would hire a full-time dockside agent.

**Mora + Alibag jetties (smaller)** — 3-8 MT/day each; mixed species.

### A4. Palghar + Thane districts

**Satpati, Dahanu, Arnala landings** — 5-12 MT/day each; pomfret +
Indian mackerel + Bombay duck. Easier community-access than Mumbai but
logistics to Purandar are longer (250+ km).

### A5. Mumbai (Sassoon Dock)

- India's largest urban fish market; 80-150 MT/day throughput.
- Complex aggregator landscape dominated by 15-20 large Mumbai traders.
- **Recommendation: do not enter Sassoon in Y1.** Too competitive; margin
  is bad for a new entrant. Revisit Y3+.

## B. Andhra Pradesh vannamei belt

### B1. West Godavari cluster (Bhimavaram, Undi, Narsapur)

- ~60,000 ha of vannamei ponds. India's single largest vannamei cluster.
- Farm-gate price (Mar 2026 VERIFY): ₹360-430/kg for 30g count; widens
  to ₹310-480/kg in crop surplus/deficit.
- **Dominant structure:** 70% of farmers take advances from integrated
  players (Avanti Feeds, CP Aquaculture India, Growel Feeds, BMR Industries
  / Nava-Fishrr). These farmers are contractually obligated to sell to
  their feed supplier at a formula-based discount (typically farm-gate
  minus 4-7%). Only the remaining ~30% of supply is "open market" for new
  entrants.
- **Our access path:**
  1. Partner with 2-3 licensed aggregators (not integrated players) who
     run truck-pickup schedules. Named candidates (VERIFY capacity + terms):
     Sri Sai Aqua, Sri Lakshmi Aqua Traders, Vijayaram Aqua Co.
  2. Offer 3-5% premium vs integrated-player formula to attract
     non-contracted farmers.
  3. Direct forward-contract with 200-300 small farmers via MPEDA's
     Farmer Producer Organisation (FPO) structure; use PM-MKSY KCC
     machinery to provide working-capital advance (8.5% interest, with
     partial govt subvention). This is the path to structural supply
     security.

### B2. East Godavari cluster (Kakinada, Razole, Amalapuram)

- ~35,000 ha; second-largest vannamei cluster. Also black tiger shrimp
  (wild + some farmed).
- Kakinada port proximity — easier export-container logistics if we ever
  need a backup route.
- Similar integrated-player dominance. Similar access pathway.

### B3. Nellore, Ongole, Prakasam clusters

- Smaller but less captured by integrated players. Farmer cooperative
  structures (named: Nellore District Fish Farmers Welfare Association)
  are more receptive to direct-contract with new aggregators.
- Farm-gate often 3-5% lower than Godavari belts.
- **Target: 30-40% of our vannamei supply from this region in Y2-Y3.**

## C. Lakshadweep tuna fishery

### C1. Lakshadweep Cooperative Marketing Federation (LCMF)

- Apex government-backed body for pole-and-line tuna aggregation.
- ~8-12 kt/yr of accessible yellowfin + skipjack, landed mainly at Agatti,
  Minicoy, Kavaratti, Kadmat.
- **Fishery status for MSC:** Lakshadweep pole-and-line fishery is
  considered among the globally sustainable tuna fisheries (similar
  profile to Maldives, which is MSC-certified). No formal MSC certification
  yet for Lakshadweep, but pre-assessment ratings from FIP-style initiatives
  have been favourable **(VERIFY current status via MSC India + DoF).**
- Pricing: yellowfin landed whole at ₹320-480/kg; skipjack ₹140-220/kg.

### C2. Named cooperatives + associations

- **Agatti Boat Owners Welfare Association** — ~80 mechanised tuna boats.
- **Minicoy Fishermen's Society** — southern-most Lakshadweep island; strong
  artisanal pole-and-line heritage.
- **Kavaratti Central Cooperative Society** — administrative hub.

### C3. Existing Indian buyers (competition for allocation)

- **Accelerated Freeze Drying (AFD), Cochin** — major historical tuna
  buyer from Lakshadweep.
- **Amalgam Foods Ltd, Cochin** — long-standing relationship with LCMF.
- **Arya Marine Products (Mangalore), Ramnagar Fisheries** — smaller
  historical quota.
- Together these likely lock 50-70% of Lakshadweep tuna. New entrant
  strategy: offer **MSC-certified premium** (unmatched by these buyers)
  in exchange for 15-20% allocation guarantee via LCMF.

### C4. Access path for us

1. Register with MPEDA as a tuna-specific exporter.
2. MoU with LCMF for annual allocation (typically negotiated in Feb-Mar for
   the following fishing season).
3. Offer 5-8% premium over LCMF's blended rate in exchange for MSC-CoC
   compliance (traceability documentation per boat per landing).
4. Fund boat-upgrade revolving scheme: ₹50-80 L for GPS + ice holds + MSC-
   compliant gear upgrades on 20-30 boats. Recoverable via catch allocation
   over 3 years.
5. Direct air link from Agatti/Minicoy to Kochi → Pune (CCJ→BOM/PNQ),
   9-11 hour chilled transit. Critical infrastructure.

## D. West Bengal mud crab aggregation

For Pillar B live mud crab:
- **Sundarbans mud crab** is the largest Indian aggregation; Canning town
  is the hub.
- **Key aggregators (VERIFY current):** Balai Lal Crab Exporters,
  Sundarban Seafood, Seabird Sea Foods Pvt Ltd (WB).
- Air logistics: CCU → BOM/DEL → onward. Transit to Purandar is ~36 hours
  (too long for live cargo); **therefore live mud crab is direct-export
  from CCU with our Mumbai office handling, not via Purandar plant.**
