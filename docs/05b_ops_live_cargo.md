# 05b · Operations — Live Cargo Playbook

v1 sold live lobster like it was e-commerce. It isn't. Every step below is
where the 3% target mortality actually comes from (vs. industry-average
8-15%).

---

## A. Species-specific handling

### A1. Spiny lobster (*Panulirus homarus / P. polyphagus*)

**From sea to tank (Konkan landing):**
1. Lobsters caught on handline / small-trap. Trapnetters lift traps within
   6-12 hr (not 24+); short-duration traps = lower stress.
2. **At the dock within 30 min of landing.** Directly into our chilled-
   aerated holding crates (salt-water, pre-cooled to 16°C).
3. Sorting: remove damaged (missing antennae ≤2, missing legs ≤1, shell
   damage ≥1 cm²) — these go to frozen-tail not live.
4. Weigh bands: A (500-650 g), B (650-850 g), C (850 g+). Live-export
   grade = B and C.

**Purging (24 hr):**
- Transfer to on-dock purging system: circulating filtered seawater, 16-
  18°C, 30-35‰ salinity, 6-8 ppm dissolved oxygen.
- Stock density ≤20 kg/m³.
- No feeding; purging removes gut contents, reduces ammonia excretion in
  transit.

**Cool-down (4-6 hr):**
- Temperature lowered gradually from 18°C to 10-14°C over 4-6 hr.
- Rapid cooling causes shock; slow cooling induces torpor (reduced
  metabolism, extended transit tolerance).

**Packing:**
- EPS (expanded polystyrene) box, 20-30 L; wall thickness 35-40 mm.
- Gel-pack ice bricks (6-8 per box; pre-frozen at -18°C; weight 250 g
  each) arranged at box perimeter.
- Bedding: **sea foam + moist shredded Sargassum / kelp**, pre-chilled
  to 8°C.
- Lobster in individual net sleeves (prevents leg entanglement).
- **Density: max 12 lobsters per box (~8-10 kg live weight).**
- Box target internal temperature at close: 10-14°C.

**Transit:**
- Cool-truck (refrigerated van, not standard reefer) from Ratnagiri/Devgad
  to Mumbai CSMIA — target 5-7 hr transit, truck set 12°C.
- Airport handling: Celebi or Bird Group perishable terminal; 1-2 hr hold
  in cold room before bellyhold loading.
- Flight: Emirates (EK501/EK503) or Qatar (QR557) BOM → DXB/DOH; 3-4 hr.
- Transit DXB/DOH hold: 2-3 hr in airport cold room.
- DXB → HKG: 7-8 hr.
- HKG arrival → importer cold room: 1-2 hr.
- **Total door-to-door: 18-24 hr target.**

**Expected mortality curves:**
| Transit total time | Mortality rate |
|---|---:|
| <16 hr | <3% |
| 16-20 hr | 3-5% |
| 20-28 hr | 5-10% |
| >28 hr | 10-18% |

### A2. Mud crab (*Scylla serrata / S. olivacea*)

- Source: West Bengal Sundarbans (larger crabs) + Konkan creek supplement.
- Packing similar EPS + gel-pack but **rubber-banded claws** to prevent
  cannibalism.
- Density: 8-10 crabs per box (1 kg+ females).
- Total door-to-door target: 22-28 hr; mortality target 4-6%.

### A3. Chilled whole pomfret (air)

- Not "live" — refrigerated; 3-5 day shelf life.
- **From dock:** ice-slurry within 20 min; pre-chilled to 1-2°C core.
- **Packing:** EPS box with crushed ice (1:1), foil-bag inner liner, waxed
  corrugate outer; 15-22 kg per box.
- Temperature in transit: 0-2°C target; bellyhold + airport cold rooms.
- Customers: HK / SG / GCC HoReCa and ethnic retail.

## B. Plant live-holding infrastructure

- 6 oxygenated holding tanks, 800-1,200 L each
  - Fibreglass or food-grade HDPE (no metal that corrodes in saltwater)
  - Closed recirculating (RAS) with biofilter + UV + protein skimmer
  - Temperature: 16-18°C (purge); separate cold room for post-cool (10-
    14°C)
  - Dissolved O2: 6-8 ppm (continuous aeration + O2 injector)
  - Ammonia monitor (critical; saltwater ammonia >0.5 ppm kills lobsters
    in hours)
- Backup power: 60 kVA DG dedicated to live-hold (zero-tolerance for
  outage)
- Capex: ₹55-65 L (v1 underestimated — true spec is higher)

## C. Air-cargo operational details

### C1. Carrier relationships (in priority order)

| Carrier | Route | Service tier | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emirates SkyCargo | BOM → DXB → HKG/SIN/TPE/NRT | "Emirates Fresh" (Dimension class) | Best live-animal protocol, dedicated cold rooms at DXB |
| Qatar Airways Cargo | BOM → DOH → HKG/NRT | QR Fresh | Good DOH transfer cold facility |
| Lufthansa Cargo | DEL/BOM → FRA → HKG | Cool/td live | Best for EU; FRA is Europe's #1 perishable hub |
| Singapore Airlines Cargo | BOM → SIN → HKG/NRT | Coolchain | Strong SIN market presence |
| Air India Cargo | BOM → SIN/HKG/NRT (direct) | Limited live-animal infrastructure | Backup only, not primary |

### C2. Documentation (72-hour pre-departure window)

Required before AWB issuance:
1. **AQCS certificate** (Animal Quarantine & Certification Service — DADF,
   Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare). Lead-time 24-48 hr from
   sample inspection.
2. **MPEDA live-export endorsement** on health certificate.
3. **Destination country import permit**:
   - HK: Food & Environmental Hygiene Dept permit; CITES NOC if species
     is listed.
   - SG: AVA (now SFA) permit.
   - Japan: MHLW import notification + 2-3 day pre-arrival docs upload.
   - Mainland China: CIQ (China Inspection & Quarantine) certificate +
     FDA registration.
4. **Commercial invoice** + packing list + country-of-origin.
5. **Airway bill (AWB)** with "LHO" service tag (Live Human Operator —
   animal care handler notified).
6. Insurance certificate (mandatory for >$10K cargo value).

### C3. Per-shipment cost structure (500 kg live lobster, BOM → HKG)

| Item | Cost (₹) | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Purging + cool-down + packing materials | 6,500 | EPS boxes, gel packs, seaweed |
| Inland cool-truck Ratnagiri → BOM | 18,000 | 5-7 hr transit |
| BOM airport handling + cold hold | 4,500 | Celebi/Bird terminal |
| Air freight BOM → HKG (Emirates, 2026 rates) | 110,000 | ~220/kg peak season; 200-240/kg spread |
| HKG handling + import clearance | 8,000 | via consignee's agent |
| Documentation (AQCS + MPEDA + carrier) | 3,000 | per-shipment |
| Insurance (3% of cargo $17,500 value ≈ ₹15 L) | 45,000 | all-risks live cargo |
| **Total landed cost at HKG** (est.) | **195,000** | ≈ ₹390/kg landed |

At FOB $28/kg × 500 kg = $14,000 = ₹12,18,000 at ₹87/USD.
After mortality (10% target) = ~450 kg live on arrival × $32 wholesale
at HKG = $14,400 → buyer margin tight but workable.

**Realistic target:** $32 wholesale at HKG * 0.92 (invoice mortality
allowance) * 500 kg = $14,720 gross revenue.

### C4. Mortality-contingency SOP

- **Survival rate <85% on arrival:** importer credit-note 20-30% of
  invoice, we absorb.
- **Survival <60%:** shipment rejected; complete loss to us; insurance
  claim initiated (paperwork takes 60-90 days).
- **Mitigation:** first 6 months shipments all under 200 kg; learn
  systematically. Graduate to 500 kg routine only after 10 clean
  shipments.
- **Quarterly mortality review:** any vessel exceeding 12% trailing 4-
  week average triggers protocol review + supplier / carrier re-audit.
