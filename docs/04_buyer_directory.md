## A. Hong Kong — Pillar B primary

### A1. Live-seafood trading houses

- **Cheung Kee Sea Products (Hong Kong) Ltd** — ~30% market share in live
  mud-crab imports into HK per trade data (VERIFY). Sai Ying Pun + Western
  Market presence; sells to specialty wet markets + HoReCa.
- **Cheung Kong Seafood Ltd** — ~24% live mud crab market share (VERIFY);
  strong southern-China onward supply.
- **Chi Ho Sea Products Company** — ~18% market share (VERIFY); specialises
  in high-end species.
- **Fat Kee Seafood Trading** — broad live seafood import (fish, crab,
  shrimp, lobster, shellfish); imports from Philippines + Indonesia; known
  buyer of seasonal premium species.
- **Worldwide Seafood Ltd** — five-star hotel grade supply; American
  lobster, Atlantic scallop already in portfolio; candidate for Indian
  spiny lobster addition.
- **Synergy Seafood Ltd** — mid-size importer + distributor.

### A2. Five-star hotel procurement (end-buyer, via importer)

- Four Seasons Hotel Hong Kong — Lobster Bar & Grill is a flagship
  lobster-buying outlet.
- Peninsula, Mandarin Oriental, Shangri-La, Grand Hyatt — flagship
  procurement through dedicated F&B buyers who source via specialty
  importers; live lobster + premium shellfish are regular purchases.

### A3. Our path into HK

1. Year 1 target: **3 supply relationships** — one from each "tier"
   (Cheung Kee or Cheung Kong for volume, Fat Kee for quality-sensitive
   mid-tier, Worldwide Seafood for hotel-grade).
2. Introduction via **HKTDC Hong Kong International Food Expo** (August)
   or Seafood Expo Asia. Start with 2 trial shipments (100-200 kg each) to
   validate mortality + grading + packaging, before term-sheeting.
3. Credit terms: first 6 months LC at sight. Negotiate to 30-day sight LC
   after 6 clean shipments.
4. HK import regulations: AQSIQ-equivalent (now SFFCS), Agriculture
   Fisheries & Conservation Department permits for live seafood; our
   MPEDA + AQCS paperwork interlocks.

## C. Japan — Pillar C primary (MSC tuna)

### C1. Sogo shosha (trading houses) with seafood divisions

- **Mitsubishi Corporation** — seafood division sources worldwide; buys
  saku + loin for Japanese retail under Seafresh brand (partly).
- **Maruha Nichiro Corporation** — Japan's largest seafood company by
  revenue (~¥1.6 trillion); has significant tuna farming operation
  (acquired Toyo Reizo's bluefin farm); still a major buyer of wild yellowfin.
- **Nissui (Nippon Suisan Kaisha)** — integrated seafood company; Nippon
  Access subsidiary distributes.
- **Kyokuyo Co.** — mid-size tuna & pelagic specialist.
- **Mitsui & Co.** — canned tuna and frozen seafood importer (more
  relevant for skipjack filler).

### C2. Japanese specialty tuna distributors

- **Ricardo Fuentes & Sons Japan** — premium bluefin specialist; mentioned
  as Japan's go-to premium tuna source. Not our direct buyer (bluefin only)
  but instructive on what "premium tuna supply" looks like.
- **True World Foods Japan** — US-origin but operates in Japan; sashimi
  distributor.
- **Issé Japan** — ikejime-method tuna specialist, small but known.
- **Toyosu Market wholesale (Tokyo)** and **Osaka Central Wholesale Market**
  — auction-based wholesale destinations for saku + loin; our Japanese
  trading house contact will route to these.

### C3. Our path into Japan

1. **MSC certification is the foot in the door.** No MSC = no real
   conversation with Maruha Nichiro / Nissui on premium tuna.
2. Attend **Japan International Seafood Show (Tokyo, August)** once MSC
   pre-assessment is positive. Visit Mitsubishi + Maruha Nichiro + Nissui
   booths; have samples shipped to their head-office QC labs ahead of the
   show.
3. Start with saku block + CO-free loin (Japan prefers CO-free for
   premium); target first commercial shipment Q3 of Y2 (if MSC timeline
   holds).
4. Credit: typically 30-45 day open account once relationship is
   established; very reliable payers. First shipments on LC at sight.

## D. Spain — Pillar C cephalopod primary

### D1. Vigo-based importers (Galicia is the octopus trading hub)

- **Nueva Pescanova, S.L.** (Redondela, Galicia) — one of the world's
  largest vertically integrated seafood companies. Octopus buyer + they
  are the first company to successfully breed octopus in captivity
  (Nueva Pescanova farm in Las Palmas). Our relevance: they buy huge raw
  tonnage for processing; relationship is pure B2B quality play.
- **Freiremar, S.A.** — Vigo; large frozen octopus importer; known for
  Moroccan sourcing historically, diversifying.
- **Pereira Group** (Pescapuerta, Pereira Productos del Mar) — Vigo; mid-
  large importer + distributor.
- **Grupo Profand** — mid-size Galician importer; growing octopus line.
- **Grupo Iberconsa** — large frozen seafood (hake + octopus + squid);
  recently changed ownership.
- **Caladero** (Zaragoza / Vigo) — large supermarket private-label
  supplier (Mercadona exclusive historically); not a direct trading-floor
  buyer but end-user of frozen octopus / squid.

### D2. Our path into Spain

1. **Conxemar (Vigo, October)** — the cephalopod/frozen-seafood trade
   fair of record. Small booth (₹10-12 L), pre-scheduled meetings with top
   8 importers.
2. Start with frozen-whole octopus (raw material grade) to prove capability.
   Upgrade to cooked + sliced in Y2 once line is installed.
3. Spain is tough on origin-transparency (Morocco-Mauritania supply
   volatility has made Spanish buyers hungry for alternates) — this is our
   structural opening. **Play "reliable Indian alternate to Morocco" story.**
4. Credit: 60-90 day open account post-relationship; first orders LC.

## E. USA — opportunistic, not primary in Y1-Y3

### E1. Major US seafood importers

- **Eastern Fish Company** (Teaneck, NJ) — leading shrimp importer with
  SAIL brand; 40-year-old operation; global sourcing for shrimp + scallops
  + crab + squid + lobster + tilapia. **Best candidate for commodity
  vannamei filler + future live-lobster.**
- **Pacific American Fish Co (PAFCO)** — Vernon, CA; established 1977;
  3,000+ fresh + frozen SKUs; retail-focused. Brands: Oceankist, Pacific
  Surf.
- **Pacific Seafood Group** — Clackamas, OR; largest integrated US seafood
  company.
- **Tradex Foods Inc** — MSC-certified global distributor (Canada/US); 40M
  lbs/yr.
- **Lusamerica Foods** — California; mid-size distributor.
- **North Coast Seafoods** — Boston; distributor + wholesaler.
- **Slade Gorton & Co., Inc.** (Boston) — oldest US seafood importer; broad
  book.

### E2. MSC + premium retail end-buyers

- **Whole Foods Market** — MSC + BAP + ASC requirements strict; major
  yellowfin tuna saku buyer.
- **Costco Wholesale** — large volumes, competitive pricing; accessible via
  dedicated importers only.
- **Trader Joe's** — private-label; MSC + sustainability-positioned.

### E3. Our path into USA

1. **Do NOT target USA in Y1-Y2.** USFDA establishment approval first
   (18-24 months post-commissioning).
2. Y2 end: soft entry via Eastern Fish for commodity shrimp filler (LC
   at sight, no retail risk).
3. Y3: post-MSC, approach Whole Foods / Trader Joe's via Tradex Foods or
   Slade Gorton as broker. Only MSC tuna saku + cooked octopus qualify.
4. **Seafood Expo North America (Boston, March)** is the single most
   important fair — do not attend until Y2 when USFDA is confirmed +
   MSC is in progress.
